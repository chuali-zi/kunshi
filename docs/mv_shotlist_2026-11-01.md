# MV shot list — Yorushika「あぶく」(2026-11-01, from real frames)
Source: /workspace/ref/abuku.mp4 (720p, 234.7 s). Frames: art/reference/mv_frames/mv_t<seconds>s.jpg (90 jpg, 480px).

## Look (applies to whole game)
- **Room**: one wide rectangular bedroom seen from a fixed, straight-on frontal camera (like a stage/diorama, ~16:9, slightly elevated, eye-level, wide lens, entire back wall visible, side walls barely visible in perspective).
- **Layout L→R**: tall dark wood bookshelf full of books (left edge); small side table w/ lamp; deep burgundy/oxblood **tufted leather wingback armchair** (front-left); back wall framed dark painting (impossible-cube/Escher-like) upper-left; **young man in tan/brown suit, round glasses, tie sits on a small wooden chair** centre against wall; power outlet; **single bed with white sheets** centre-right; small nightstand + white vase/plant; round **wall clock** above bed; **pendant lamp** hanging from ceiling centre; **door** in right wall (opens inward, dark beyond) where the killer enters.
- **Palette**: cream/beige wallpaper with small red-rose floral pattern, warm yellow tungsten light, deep **forest-green carpet**, burgundy chair, dark brown wood, white bed. Strong vignette, dark ceiling/edges, film-grain, slight desaturation, warm amber grade.
- **Killer**: figure in long black leather/rubber coat, black hood/balaclava + hat, gloves, pistol with bright muzzle flash.
- **Lights-out state** (t≈84–123 s): room goes near-monochrome grey/blue-black, only muzzle flashes/flashlight cone; killer silhouetted.
- Subtitles small white text bottom-centre; title 「あぶく」 brush calligraphy white, upper-centre/right.

## Sequence (death loop)
1. 0–6 s: man sits on chair; killer opens right door, shoots → man dies. Title card.
2. 9–30 s: loop resets (man back on chair). Man tries options: stands, grabs a stick/chair, swings; killer still kills. Repeated deaths.
3. 30–42 s: hand-to-hand struggle centre; a shot breaks the painting/wall (glass-crack impact decal).
4. ~45 s: comic/anime cut-in (manga-style close-up of the man's glasses face) — "realisation" beat.
5. 48–80 s: more loops; man experiments with bed, chair, bookshelf; lies on bed; hides.
6. 84–123 s: lights off — monochrome stealth, flashlight beam, gunfight in the dark, body on floor.
7. 129–160 s: lights back; man prepares (climbs, poses near door, waits beside door).
8. ~158 / 188 s: split-screen grid of many loops (2x2 / 4x4 tiled rooms) — montage of attempts.
9. 190–200 s: close-ups, man looks at camera; **bullets crack the "screen" (4th wall glass shatter)** repeatedly.
10. 200–220 s: camera finally breaks out — handheld **first-person** view, man wrestles killer at close range, handheld shaky angles, tilted Dutch angles of room, painting.
11. 220–231 s: first-person fight, muzzle flash in face; final shot: own empty hands over green carpet, then lying on bed looking at the door/killer. Credits on black.

## Implications for the game
- Default camera = fixed frontal wide diorama exactly like frame mv_t0006.0s.jpg.
- Dark/lights-off mode, screen-crack effect, manga cut-in, split-screen loop montage, and late-game first-person breakout are signature beats.
